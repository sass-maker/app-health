## Honest limits

Session counts are not unique people. Hashes are scoped to project/environment;
legacy events without hashes do not acquire historical sessions retroactively.
Sampled session counts are labeled lower bounds. Historical projection/cache
refresh can lag ingestion; live sessions use separate short-lived presence.

This release covers the integration foundation and current 1-hour/24-hour
analytics. Longer date ranges, campaign/device/geography breakdowns, funnels,
revenue attribution, exports, and archive replay remain follow-up capabilities.
Swift is locally verified but not yet published as a remotely installable package.
No other Fleet product was instrumented during this release.
